5-Step Sourcing Framework

How Our Recruitment Process Works

A transparent, structured workflow designed to deliver qualified candidates faster and eliminate hiring friction.

Step 01

Understand the Role

We consult closely with your hiring managers to define key responsibilities, mandatory skill sets, salary parameters, reporting lines, and the personality traits that will thrive in your team culture.

Step 02

Source Candidates

We tap into our vetted talent network, premium regional job boards, and executive talent pools to engage both actively searching candidates and high-caliber passive industry professionals.

Step 03

Screen & Assess

Our experienced recruiters conduct rigorous CV reviews, initial screening interviews, technical competencies checks, salary alignment, and availability/visa status verifications.

Step 04

Shortlist & Interview

You receive a targeted shortlist of the top 3–5 pre-vetted candidates with recruiter assessment notes. We handle all interview scheduling and candidate feedback to respect your calendar.

Step 05

Offer & Onboarding

We assist in offer preparation, compensation negotiation, and seamless handover to our in-house PRO & visa team to secure work permits and visas without unnecessary delays.
