Frequently Asked Questions About HR & PRO Services in the UAE

Everything you need to know about outsourcing HR, visas, payroll, and government compliance in Dubai and across the UAE.

PRO (Public Relations Officer) services cover the government paperwork every UAE business needs — employment visas, Emirates ID, labour cards, licence renewals, document attestation and approvals from authorities such as MoHRE, ICP and GDRFA. Hyperscale.ae handles these processes on your behalf so you don't have to visit government offices.

For startups and SMEs, a full in-house HR team is often expensive and underused. Outsourcing gives you experienced HR, payroll and PRO specialists for a fraction of the cost, with the flexibility to scale support up or down as your team grows.

The Wage Protection System (WPS) is the UAE's electronic salary transfer system, monitored by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE). Mainland companies registered with MoHRE must pay employee salaries through WPS via approved banks or exchange houses. Late or missed WPS payments can lead to fines and restrictions on issuing new work permits. Our payroll service keeps you compliant every month.

Timelines depend on the emirate, licence type, nationality of the employee and document readiness, but a standard employment visa usually takes around two to four weeks from entry permit to Emirates ID. With complete documents and an experienced PRO team, we help avoid the most common delays.

Under UAE labour law, gratuity for full-time private sector employees is based on basic salary and length of service — generally 21 days' basic pay for each of the first five years and 30 days' basic pay for each additional year, subject to legal limits. We calculate and process gratuity accurately as part of our payroll and HR operations services.
