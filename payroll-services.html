MoHRE Compliance Framework

What Is WPS and Why It Matters

The Wage Protection System (WPS) is the UAE's electronic salary transfer system, monitored by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE) and the Central Bank of the UAE. It ensures employees are paid on time, in full, and strictly in accordance with their registered labour contracts.

Mainland companies registered with MoHRE must pay salaries through WPS-approved banks or exchange houses. Non-compliance, late files, or incorrect salary amounts can lead to severe penalties, work permit freezes, and downgrading of your company's classification tier. Our payroll team makes sure your WPS submissions are accurate, fully validated, and submitted well before deadlines.
