Regulatory Insight

Mainland and Free Zone? We Handle Both

Visa and labour processes depend heavily on where your company is registered. We know every technical nuance across all emirates.

Mainland Companies

Mainland entities fall under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE) and the respective emirate's immigration department (e.g. GDRFA Dubai or ICP Sharjah).

  • MoHRE electronic work permit and quota clearance
  • Mandatory WPS (Wage Protection System) registration
  • Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 compliance
  • Emiratisation target tracking & records

Free Zone Companies

Free zone businesses process visas directly through their specific Free Zone Authority (such as DMCC, DIFC, DAFZA, Shams, IFZA, Meydan, or JAFZA) with authority-specific regulations.

  • Authority-specific visa quotas and portals
  • Custom employment contracts and internal free zone rules
  • Free zone electronic residence permits
  • Direct authority liaison without general typing centre visits
